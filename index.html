The science

A behavioral framework, not a marketing plan

Our approach is built on the 7 steps of human behavior change — the same sequence neuroscientists use to understand how beliefs form, shift, and lock in.

The 7 Steps of Brand Behavior Change
How humans actually move from stranger to advocate
01

Interrupt the Pattern

Cut through the noise. The brain filters 99% of stimuli. Your brand needs to trigger the orienting response — the neurological "wait, what?" moment.

02

Create Comfort

Once you have attention, the amygdala asks: safe or threat? Build instant trust signals that shift the nervous system from guarded to receptive.

03

Lead the Imagination

The prefrontal cortex lights up when people imagine alternate futures. Show them a better reality — one where your brand is part of their world.

04

Shift the Feeling

Emotion drives decision. Not the other way around. Create the feeling first — excitement, relief, belonging — then the logical mind follows.

05

Satisfy the Critical Mind

Now give them logical permission to act on the emotion they already feel. Data, frameworks, social proof — the rational alibi for an emotional decision.

06

Change the Association

Repetition + emotion = rewired neural pathways. Your brand stops being "a company" and becomes "the way we think about this problem."

07

Trigger Action

Physical engagement locks in the new association. A click, a sign-up, a conversation. The body commits what the mind decided.
