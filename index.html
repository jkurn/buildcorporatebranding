The 7 Steps of Brand Behavior Change
How humans actually move from stranger to advocate
01

Interrupt the Pattern

The brain filters 99% of stimuli. Your brand triggers the orienting response — the neurological "wait, what?" moment that earns attention.

02

Create Comfort

The amygdala asks: safe or threat? Trust signals shift the nervous system from guarded to receptive in milliseconds.

03

Lead the Imagination

The prefrontal cortex lights up when people imagine alternate futures. Show them a better reality with your brand in it.

04

Shift the Feeling

Emotion drives decision — not the other way around. Create the feeling first. The logical mind follows.

05

Satisfy the Critical Mind

Now give logical permission to act on the emotion. Data, proof, frameworks — the rational alibi for an emotional decision.

06

Change the Association

Repetition + emotion = rewired neural pathways. Your brand stops being "a company" and becomes the way they think about the problem.

07

Trigger Action

Physical engagement locks in the new association. A click, a sign-up, a conversation. The body commits what the mind decided.
